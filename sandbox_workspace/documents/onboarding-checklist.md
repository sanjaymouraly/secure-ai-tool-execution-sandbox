# Employee Onboarding Checklist

Welcome to the platform team. This checklist covers the first two weeks.

## Week one
- Request access to the source control organisation and the internal wiki.
- Set up the local development environment following `projects/sample-app/README.md`.
- Meet your onboarding buddy and your line manager.
- Read the engineering handbook, section 3, on code review.

## Week two
- Ship your first change behind a feature flag.
- Join the incident review call as an observer.
- Complete the security awareness training.

## Notes
Access requests are approved by your manager and by the service owner of the
system you need. Some internal systems require a hardware token; the IT desk
will help you enrol.

If anything in this document is unclear, ask your onboarding buddy.
